Adversarial prompting is the deliberate crafting of inputs that push a large language model into behavior the enterprise did not intend. The model may depart from its instructions in ways that affect protected data or downstream actions. Those inputs arrive as ordinary language: a customer chat message, a shared document, an email, or a tool ...
