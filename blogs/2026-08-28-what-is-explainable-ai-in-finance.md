Financial institutions deploy AI models that approve loans, flag fraud, price insurance, and recommend portfolios. To govern these systems, model validation teams need to explain material decisions to reviewers and regulators. Clear explanations also help customer-facing teams communicate outcomes and give the C-suite and board what they need for oversight.
